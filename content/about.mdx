# About

Hi, I'm Harsh — a student and developer from India.

Most of what I build lives on the web. I like TypeScript, small tools that do
one thing properly, and reading other people's source code, which has taught me
more than any tutorial.

## What I'm doing

I'm working through the fundamentals properly rather than collecting frameworks:
data structures, how the browser actually works, and why the abstractions I use
every day exist. Alongside that I ship side projects, because nothing exposes a
gap in understanding faster than trying to deploy something.

## What I write about

The things that took me longest to understand. If I had to piece an explanation
together from six tabs and a Stack Overflow answer from 2016, it probably
deserves a post.
